**Subject: Our new roster app — Myrosta (sign in on any device)**

Hi team,

We've moved the ED roster off the spreadsheet and onto **Myrosta** — a simple, secure web app you use from your phone or computer. Your shifts, self‑rostering, leave and swaps, all in one place.

**To get in (takes a minute):**

1. On your phone, open **https://myrosta.com.au** and tap **Sign in**.
2. Leave it on **📱 Mobile (SMS)**, enter your mobile, tap **Send code**. *(Prefer email? Tap **✉️ Email** instead.)*
3. Type the **6‑digit code** you receive → you're in.

You **stay signed in** on that device — no password, and you won't have to log in again each time.

*(Prefer to scan? There's a one‑page handout with a **QR code** that opens Myrosta — on the staff board, or ask me for it.)*

**Add it to your home screen** so it opens like an app:
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → *Add to Home Screen*
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*

**On a computer:** go to **https://myrosta.com.au** in Chrome, Edge or Safari and sign in the same way (a code by SMS or email). In Chrome/Edge you can click the **install icon** in the address bar to keep it as a desktop app — everything stays in sync with your phone.

**What you can do:**
- See **just your own shifts** — list or calendar — and push them to your phone calendar with reminders.
- **Pick your shifts** when a round opens (it's collision‑proof and quota‑fair).
- **Request leave or a swap** in a tap.

**Code not arriving?** Check junk/spam, wait a moment and tap **Resend**, and make sure I've got your correct mobile/email on file. If your hospital email blocks it, just use **Mobile (SMS)**. Any trouble — message me.
