# Myrosta — staff emails (next-cycle pilot)

Parallel-run pilot for the next roster cycle (**3 August – 27 September 2026**). Existing system stays the official roster; staff also self-roster in Myrosta to trial it. Self-roster round opens **Monday 29 June 2026**.

---

## 1. Launch email

**Subject:** Try our new self-rostering app for the next cycle — no risk, your roster doesn't change

Hi team,

You all know the pain: hunting through a 56-column spreadsheet to find your own shifts, leave requests lost in email, and no easy way to get your roster onto your phone. I've built something to fix that — **Myrosta**, a simple web app made *by* one of us, *for* our department.

**What it is.** One place — on any phone or computer — to see your own shifts, add them to your calendar, pick your shifts when rostering opens, and request leave or swaps. It's live at **myrosta.com.au**.

**How the pilot works (the important bit).** We're trialling Myrosta on the **next roster cycle (3 August – 27 September 2026)**, and **nothing about your actual roster changes.** We'll keep running the existing system exactly as we do now — that remains the official roster, and it's what counts for your shifts and your pay.

**Alongside it, in parallel, you'll also use Myrosta to enter your own shifts** for that cycle — purely to get a feel for self-rostering and tell me what you think. It's a no-pressure trial running next to the real thing. If the app ever shows something different from the official roster, the official roster wins — this cycle, Myrosta is just for the experience and your feedback.

**What I'd love you to do (≈5–10 minutes):**

1. Open **myrosta.com.au** and tap **Sign in**.
2. Choose **📱 Mobile (SMS)** or **✉️ Email** and enter the number/address we have on file → you'll get a **6-digit code** (no password, ever).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen** so it opens just like an app.
4. The **self-roster round opens Monday 29 June** — head to **Self-roster** and **pick your own shifts** for the 3 Aug – 27 Sep cycle.
5. Tell me what works and what doesn't — there's a **💬 Send feedback** button right inside the Help tab.

**Your data is safe.** Sign-in is passwordless and limited to our team only; everything is hosted securely in Australia (Sydney); there's no clinical or patient data anywhere near it; and your work email and payroll number are kept private. On a shared ward computer, just tick *"This is a shared computer"* at sign-in and it logs you out when you're done.

This is genuinely **ours** — it changes based on what you tell me. Give it an honest go over the next cycle, and let's see if it makes the roster something we actually enjoy using.

Thanks,
**Dr Ranjith Kandavelu**
Emergency Department · Werribee Mercy
*Questions? Just reply, or message me inside the app.*
