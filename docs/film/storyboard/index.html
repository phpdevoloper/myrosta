Myrosta: the film

A roster that lives on the phone in your pocket, is fair to the people on it, and takes minutes, not nights. Thirteen frames, one cycle, two people, at the fictional Wattle Hospital emergency department. Both sides of the roster: the staff member who chooses, the rota lead who still decides.

How it was madeEach frame animated with Google Veo 3.1 from its keyframe (the lines are spoken on camera; the handover crowd and the garden on Kling 3.0). The master opens on a hook card, and the swap approval is a composite of two real phone recordings on one plate. Every spoken line was then checked for lip movement as well as timing (a face-aware measure plus a frame-by-frame mouth strip); the café line and the office exchange were re-generated because their first takes smiled or grinned through the words. Narration: an Australian stock AI voice (Soraya). Music: generated. App screens: real recordings of the demo sandbox. Assembly: ffmpeg from a timeline file in docs/film/gen/film, at the clips’ native 24 fps, with the lip sync of every spoken line measured against mouth movement (every line measured within about 40 ms; no clip needed a shift).
