# Myrosta run-through videos — chapter scripts v1, for approval

*7 October 2026. Approved plan ("all defaults"): real screens, first person, no characters in the run-through, shots from the walkthrough film as chapter openers only; one film for staff, one for admins, a three-minute director's cut; the narrator is the film's voice (Soraya); captions burned in; MP4s plus a chapter page beside the guides, and 9:16 staff chapters for sharing by message. Core chapters for every site, module chapters per group; the intro cards are tailored for SWIFT first. Sites that book in rounds (the first ED) and sites that run a rolling roster (SWIFT) get their own booking chapter; everything else is shared.*

**How to read a chapter.** The card is the time-of-day title that opens it. "You see" is the screen the viewer is looking at. "You do" is the exact taps, with the app's own labels. "Narration" is what Soraya says, in the second person, timed to the taps; it is the text to approve. "The rule" is the one thing the chapter teaches that a guide would make you read twice. Lengths are targets; the narration is written for them.

**What is reused.** Seven recordings from the film are reused as they are or extended: the booking (R2), the month view and Add to calendar (R2b), the grid filling (R3), the swap (R4), the approval (R5), the day board, fairness and audit log (R6), the publish and the notification (R7), the leave request and its card (R8). Everything else is new, recorded in the demo sandbox with its synthetic people (Emma Walsh, Sam Patel, Alex Campbell, Morgan O'Brien) and the fictional Wattle Hospital.

**The conventions on screen.** A tap ripple on the phone, a cursor glide on the laptop, a short zoom on the control that matters, a step label in the corner ("2 · Tap a zone marked Open"), a time-of-day card at each chapter start, captions throughout. The first three seconds of every chapter work with the sound off.

## Film 1 · Myrosta for staff: your roster, first hand

Phone throughout. Core about six minutes; modules add thirty to fifty seconds each for the groups that have them switched on.

### S0 · Opening (15 s)

**Card.** Your roster, in your pocket. Seven things, six minutes.
**Opener.** The handover shot from the film, every phone buzzing at once (3 s, muted).
**Narration.** "This is Myrosta on your phone. Seven things you'll do with it, from the first text to your next leave request. We'll do each one together, as it happens."

### S1 · Day one: sign in, and put it on your Home Screen (40 s)

**Card.** Day one, 7:40 am · an invite arrives.
**You see.** A text: your manager has added you to Myrosta, with the link. The sign-in page at myrosta.com.au.
**You do.** Tap the link · choose Mobile (SMS) · enter your mobile · tap Text me a code · type the 6-digit code · you're in, on My shifts. Then Safari's Share · Add to Home Screen.
**Narration.** "Your invite comes as a text or an email. Tap the link, choose mobile or email, and ask for a code. There's no password: the code is the password, and this phone stays signed in. You land on My shifts. One more thing, once: Share, then Add to Home Screen, so Myrosta opens like an app."
**The rule.** No code? Check junk mail, tap Resend, or use SMS if a hospital filter eats email. Still nothing: your admin checks the number Myrosta has for you.
**Capture.** The sign-in page on a local copy of the app with the code request intercepted and answered by the recorder, so nothing real is sent and no real code is typed; a synthetic number. Add to Home Screen in the iOS Simulator's Safari, or a three-step card if the simulator will not play.

### S2a · The round opens: book your share (rounds sites, 75 s)

**Card.** Monday, 8:05 am · the round opens.
**Opener.** Emma at the café window, from the film (3 s, muted).
**You see.** The text: booking is open until Friday. Book shifts, a day tapped open, the day sheet.
**You do.** Tap Book shifts · tap a day · choose AM or PM · tap a zone marked Open · tap another day · tap Confirm · the receipt.
**Narration.** "The text says the round is open. Open Book shifts and tap a day. At the top, AM or PM. Below it, one chip per zone: Open means it's yours to take, and a taken one shows who has it. Your picks wait in the basket at the bottom. Add a few more days, then Confirm. The receipt is the thing to look at: that's your share, booked. Your share is set by your admin, per fortnight, and the counter shows how much of it you've used. If a colleague confirmed the same slot a moment before you, you're told which pick didn't go in, and you pick again. When the round closes, your admin publishes, and the whole roster lands on your phone."
**The rule.** Up to your share, nobody picks for you, and the first to confirm keeps the slot.
**Capture.** R2 as recorded, extended with a second day and the Fn counter in frame.

### S2b · A rolling roster: book what's open, claim what's posted (rolling sites, 75 s)

**Card.** Any day · the roster is live.
**Opener.** The same café shot (3 s, muted).
**You see.** Book shifts with the manager's placements already on it; a day with an open zone; Open shifts on My shifts.
**You do.** Tap Book shifts · tap a day · choose the type · tap a zone marked Open · Confirm · the receipt "Booked 2 · 9 of 30". Then My shifts · Open shifts · Claim.
**Narration.** "Your roster is live from launch week and live all cycle. Your manager places much of each fortnight; you book what is still open, whenever you need to, within your share. Tap a day, choose the shift type, tap a zone marked Open, and Confirm. The receipt counts your share. When your manager posts a gap, or a colleague gives a shift away, it appears under Open shifts on My shifts and in your notifications. Tap Claim: the first person free to take it gets it, with the same checks as any booking. Days that have passed are locked."
**The rule.** Book what's open within your share; claim what's posted; past days are locked.
**Capture.** New: the sandbox switched to Rolling, the marketplace on, one open shift broadcast.

### S3 · Your shifts, your calendar, your reminder (45 s)

**Card.** Monday evening · where your shifts live now.
**You see.** My shifts: your next shift, the next 7 days, the whole cycle, the month view. Calendar: Add to calendar, Live calendar. A reminder text.
**You do.** Tap My shifts · scroll the next 7 days · open Whole cycle · Month · tap Calendar · Live calendar · Copy (or Open on iPhone) · Next 7 days · Share.
**Narration.** "My shifts is your week at a glance: the next shift, the next seven days, the whole cycle, and your counts. Tap Calendar. Add to calendar puts the dates you choose in your phone's calendar; Live calendar gives you a link that keeps itself up to date, so it sits in the calendar your family already shares. And the evening before each shift, Myrosta texts you, in case the week ran away. Share sends your week as a message."
**The rule.** Live calendar updates itself; Add to calendar is a snapshot.
**Capture.** R2b as recorded plus the Live calendar sheet and the Share sheet; the reminder text as a phone-notification still.

### S4 · Swap a shift (70 s)

**Card.** Two weeks later, Thursday 1:40 pm · the school rings.
**Opener.** Emma in the corridor on the phone, from the film (3 s, muted).
**You see.** Book shifts → the Saturday → Swap it. With a colleague / Ask everyone. Requests → Asking everyone → the offers. The other side: Swaps wanted → Offer a shift.
**You do.** Tap the shift · Swap it · Ask everyone · add a note · Ask everyone. Later: Requests · Asking everyone · Accept · confirm. As the colleague: Requests · Swaps wanted · choose one of your shifts · Send offer.
**Narration.** "Something's come up for Saturday. Tap the shift, then Swap it. Two ways: with a colleague, where you pick them and one of their shifts and they agree first; or Ask everyone. The screen says how many colleagues it goes to: those free that day who have a shift you could take. Add a note, and ask. Offers arrive under Requests. Accept the one you want; the others close by themselves, and it goes to your admin, who approves it with the cover in front of them. If a colleague asks, Swaps wanted lists the asks you could take: choose one of your shifts and send the offer."
**The rule.** Every swap ends with your admin approving it; an ask or an offer closes itself if its shift changes.
**Capture.** R4 and R5 as recorded; add the Swaps wanted side from Alex's phone.

### S5 · When you can't work a shift (50 s)

**Card.** Nine days out · plans change.
**You see.** The day sheet of a shift more than the notice period away: Remove this shift. One inside it: Offer it to the group, Ask to come off, Swap it, and the notice left. Mark unavailable (UA) on a free day.
**You do.** Tap the far shift · Remove this shift. Tap the near shift · Offer it to the group. Then Ask to come off · a reason · send. On a free day: Mark unavailable (UA).
**Narration.** "Further out than your group's notice period, you can simply remove a shift: it goes straight to the group as an open shift. Inside the notice period the day sheet tells you how many days are left, and gives you three ways: offer it to the group, and you're off when a colleague takes it; ask to come off with a reason, and your admin decides; or swap it. You can do all three, and whichever happens first wins. A day you can't work at all: mark it unavailable, so nobody expects you. That isn't leave: it never reaches pay, and a shift you already hold that day stays until you swap it or offer it."
**The rule.** Unavailable is not leave.
**Capture.** New, in the sandbox with the notice period set to 7 days.

### S6 · Leave (40 s)

**Card.** The school holidays.
**Opener.** Emma on the sofa with her phone is not in the film, so none; the chapter opens on the request sheet.
**You see.** Requests → Request leave: dates, type, reason, Send. The request in Your requests; then Approved.
**You do.** Requests · Request leave · From, To · Type · reason · Send leave request. Later: the request reads Approved.
**Narration.** "Requests, then Request leave: the dates, the type, a reason, send. Your admin sees the whole picture, the leave you've taken, who else is away, the cap, and approves or declines; if they ask a question, the request says it needs your reply. Once approved, any shift you had those days comes off and is offered to the group. You'll know before lunch."
**The rule.** Approved leave frees your shifts for the group; nothing to chase.
**Capture.** R8, Emma's half.

### S7 · Staying in the loop (30 s)

**Card.** Every day.
**You see.** The bell and its sheet; Get open-shift alerts on this phone; Day board; Fairness; Help → Send feedback.
**You do.** Tap the bell · an open-shift alert · Day board · Fairness · More · Help · Send feedback.
**Narration.** "The bell tells you about approvals, swaps, open shifts and messages; switch on alerts and they reach your lock screen. Day board shows who's on today and where. Fairness shows everyone the same table of who carries the weekends and evenings. And Help sends your question straight to the people who built it."
**End card.** Questions: your admin · The staff guide: [link] · Myrosta.

### Staff module chapters (recorded separately; used where the group has the feature on)

**M3 · Your own hours (35 s).** Card: a later start. You do: book a zone · the Hours row · change the start · Confirm. On a booked shift: Change hours · Save · Same as the type. **M4 · Who's on across the department (30 s).** Card: Wednesday handover. You do: Day board · Whole department · a group card · Print. ## Film 2 · Myrosta for admins: a cycle, first hand

Laptop for settings, the grid and the board; phone for the moments that happen on a phone. Core about nine minutes with one booking-mode chapter; modules add forty to sixty seconds each.

### A0 · Opening (20 s)

**Card.** You run the roster. A whole cycle, first hand.
**Opener.** Sam at the doctors' station with the grid filling, from the film (3 s, muted).
**Narration.** "You run the roster. This is a whole cycle in Myrosta, as you'll live it: set it up, invite your people, open it, run it day to day, and pay it. Nine minutes, your hands on it the whole time."

### A1 · The cycle and its settings (90 s)

**Card.** Three weeks before the cycle · Settings.
**You see.** Settings → This cycle, card by card; then This staff group.
**You do.** Settings · This cycle · Status · Dates (start, length) · Zones · Cover (min and max per half, Save cover) · Shift types (rename AM and PM, + Add a shift type, Both halves counts two, Save shift types) · Notice to come off (days, Save notice period) · Quota & fairness (share, per fortnight) · Mode & method (Rounds or Rolling). This staff group · Group (name, logo, Features: Open-shift marketplace) · Open-shift marketplace (First-come wins or Admin approves).
**Narration.** "Everything about a cycle lives on the cycle, so the next one starts from a copy and nothing you set now leaks into the past. Dates first. Zones: one slot per half-day each. Cover: the minimum and maximum per half. Shift types: rename AM and PM, add the rest; a type that fills both halves is a full shift and counts two toward a share. Notice to come off: inside it, staff can't simply drop a shift. Quota and fairness: each person's share, per fortnight. Mode: rounds, where everyone books in a window and you publish; or rolling, live once and live all cycle. Then the group's own card: its name, its logo, and the open-shift marketplace, so a shift someone drops reaches the whole group. Get started in Settings shows anything still unticked."
**The rule.** Settings sit on the cycle; a type with bookings can't change its half, so switch it off and add another.
**Capture.** New, laptop.

### A2 · People and access (60 s)

**Card.** Two weeks before · Staff.
**You see.** Staff tab: a person's card (name, mobile, email, role, quota, tags). Access control: Sync allow-list, Invite your staff, Make admin. Import data: Staff list (.xlsx).
**You do.** Staff · + Add · name, mobile or email, role, share · Save. Settings · This staff group · Access control · Sync allow-list from staff + admins · Invite your staff · Send to … by text. Make admin for a colleague. Import data · Staff list (.xlsx) for a whole group at once.
**Narration.** "Every person needs a mobile or an email: that's how they sign in and how reminders reach them. Add them one by one, or import the staff list from a spreadsheet. Access control decides who may sign in: sync the allow-list, then Invite your staff. The invites are real texts and emails, so send them when you mean it. Make admin gives a colleague the same keys as you."
**The rule.** No mobile or email, no sign-in, no reminders.
**Capture.** New, laptop; the invite step stops at the confirm so nothing is sent.

### A3a · Rounds: open the round, watch it fill, publish (rounds sites, 75 s)

**Card.** Monday, 8:05 am · the round opens.
**Opener.** The handover shot (3 s, muted).
**You see.** Booking tab: Open booking; the Roster grid filling over the week; red "needs cover", Show gaps; Close booking; Publish; the notification on a staff phone; a cell edited afterwards with a reason; the audit log.
**You do.** Booking · Open booking · confirm. (Days pass.) Roster · Show gaps · a short day. Booking · Close booking · Publish · confirm. A staff phone: the bell. Roster · a cell · change · reason · Save · Audit log.
**Narration.** "Open the round, and the roster lands on every phone at once. Then you watch. The grid fills itself over the week, and a red tag shows any half that's still short. When the round closes, publish: everyone's told, and that's the roster of record. You can still change any cell afterwards; on a live roster each change asks for a reason and is written to the audit log with your name and the time."
**The rule.** Publish once; edit with a reason; the log remembers.
**Capture.** R3 and R7 as recorded, plus Open booking and the cell edit, laptop.

### A3b · Rolling: place, fill the gaps, go live (rolling sites, 75 s)

**Card.** A week before launch · Booking.
**You see.** The cell editor; Pre-fill the rest; Short slots; Broadcast open shift; the booking window on the Go live step; Rolling roster → Go live.
**You do.** Roster · tap a cell · Shift · type · zone · Hours if needed · Save. Booking · Pre-fill the rest · Fill to minimum cover · check. Roster · needs cover · Short slots · Assign… or Broadcast. Booking · Go live step · Opens, Closes, on · Save schedule (optional). Rolling roster · Go live.
**Narration.** "On a rolling roster you place first. Tap a cell: off, shift or leave, the type, the zone, and the real hours if they differ from the type. Pre-fill the rest places people fairly into every short half, keeping to the rules; check it before you trust it. Gaps show as needing cover: assign someone, or broadcast the slot as an open shift for the group to claim. If you want to place everything yourself for a while, set a booking window before you go live. Then Go live: everyone's told, the app is the record, and the spreadsheet is never imported again."
**The rule.** From Go live the app is the record.
**Capture.** New, laptop, the sandbox in Rolling.

### A4 · Requests (90 s)

**Card.** Thursday, 2:05 pm · three things to decide.
**Opener.** Sam walking the corridor, one tap, from the film (3 s, muted).
**You see.** Requests: a swap via Ask everyone with the two shifts side by side; an ask to come off with the notice left, the reason, the cover without them and Free that day; a leave request with its card: EFT, hours per fortnight, confirmed leave this year, this request in days and hours, who else is away, the cap.
**You do.** Requests · the swap · Approve. The ask to come off · Free that day · a tap-to-call · Let them off. The leave request · read the card · Approve (or Ask a question).
**Narration.** "Requests is where the roster asks you things, each with what you need to decide. A swap arrives once the colleague has agreed, or once an ask-everyone offer has been accepted: the two shifts side by side, approve or decline, and the roster moves. An ask to come off shows the notice left, the reason, the cover without them, and everyone free that day with a tap-to-call; keep them on, or let them off and the gap goes to the group. A leave request comes with the whole picture: their EFT, the leave they've taken, this request in hours, who else is away, and the cap. Approve, decline, or ask a question, which comes back with their reply. Approving takes their shifts off those days and offers each to the group."
**The rule.** Staff choose; you still decide, with the cover in front of you.
**Capture.** R5 and R8 as recorded (phone); the ask to come off new, laptop.

## Production notes

- **Two booking modes.** Each site's staff film takes S2a or S2b and each admin film A3a or A3b; the chapter page shows the one that applies.
- **Intro cards per site.** SWIFT first: the group's name and first-cycle dates on the S0 and A0 cards, from the site folder, outside the repo.
- **Names.** Only the sandbox's synthetic people appear on screen; the guides' named admins become "your admin" or "your manager" in every line.
- **Whole department.** The only screen the sandbox cannot show. Twenty seconds captured by you on a signed-in multi-group account (laptop for A7, phone for M4), or a still.
- **Sign-in.** Recorded against a local copy with the code request intercepted and answered by the recorder; nothing is sent, no real code typed, a synthetic number.
- **Lengths.** Staff core about 6 min; admin core about 9 min with one mode chapter; director's cut 3 min; modules 35 to 60 s each. Narration is written at about 1.8 words a second to leave room for the taps.
- **Delivery.** Per film: 1080p MP4 captioned, a 720p copy; per chapter: its own MP4 and, for the staff chapters, a 9:16 version; a chapter page beside each guide with every chapter as a player and a link to the guide's section.
- **Credits.** Narration only, about 75 lines, roughly 25 credits. No new AI video.
