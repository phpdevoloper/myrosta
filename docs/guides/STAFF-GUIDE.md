# Myrosta — Staff Guide

*Your shifts, self‑rostering, leave and swaps — on any device.*

Myrosta is our Emergency Department roster app. It shows you your own shifts, lets you pick them fairly when rostering opens, and handles leave and swaps — all from your phone or computer. This is your quick guide.

## Signing in (just once)
1. Open **https://myrosta.com.au** and tap **Sign in**.
2. Choose **📱 Mobile (SMS)** (the default) and enter your mobile — or tap **✉️ Email** — then **Send code**.
3. Type the **6‑digit code** you receive (by text or email) → **Verify**.
4. You're in — and you **stay signed in** on that device, so you won't have to do this again.

> **On a shared or ward computer?** Tick **This is a shared computer** at sign‑in — it signs you out when you close it or after a short idle. On your own phone, leave it unticked.

**Add it to your home screen** so it opens like an app:
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share button → *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → *Add to Home screen*.

> **No code?** Check your junk/spam folder, wait a moment then tap **Resend code**, and make sure you used the mobile or email your admin has on file. **Hospital blocking external mail?** Just use **📱 Mobile (SMS)**, or ask your admin to add a **personal email**. Still stuck → message your roster admin.

## Your shifts — "My shifts"
- See **only your own** shifts, as a **list** or a **month** calendar (weekends are shaded).
- **Locum shifts** (ones you cover on top of your roster) show in **pink with an ‘L’**, and in your shift tally.
- Tap **Add to calendar** to push your shifts — with reminders — straight into your phone's calendar (Apple/Google/Outlook/Teams).

## Booking your shifts — "Self‑roster"
When the admin opens a rostering round, go to **Self‑roster** and pick your shifts.
- You only ever see slots that are **still free** — the moment someone takes one it disappears, so **two people can never book the same shift**.
- Your progress shows against your **quota** (how many shifts you owe, usually counted per fortnight to match pay).
- Tap a slot to **book** it; tap your own booked slot to **release** it (while the round is still open).

How the round runs is set by your admin — you might see:
- **In random groups** — you're placed in a random group and the groups book in turn; when your group is picking, book your shifts, then tap **I'm done — pass my remaining picks**.
- **Take turns** — everyone picks one at a time, and the order reverses each round so no one is always last.
- **Everyone at once** — open booking, with fairness rules so nobody races ahead of their share.
- **Pre-filled, then adjust** — the app drafts a fair roster and you tweak or swap where needed.

However it's run, fairness rules and quotas mean **the last person to log in still gets a fair go**.

## Requesting leave
1. **Requests → Request leave**.
2. Pick your **dates** and the **type** (Annual, CME, Long service, Parental, Public holiday…), add a note if you like → submit.
3. The admin reviews it with full context and decides. You're **notified**, and it updates the roster and leave planner automatically.

> **Off sick? (unplanned leave)** Choose **UPL — Unplanned leave** for the date(s). Once your admin actions it, your shift comes off the roster and the gap is visible for a colleague to pick up as **locum** cover.

> **Tip:** check the **Leave planner** first to pick days when fewer colleagues are away — those are likelier to be approved.

## Swapping a shift
1. **Requests → Request a swap**.
2. Choose **your shift**, the **colleague**, and **their shift** you'd like to swap into → submit.
3. Once the admin approves, both rosters update.

## The Leave planner
A colour‑coded view of the **whole team's leave for the year** — so you can see who's away and plan around it.

## If you work a fixed pattern
Some consultants have a set pattern (e.g. every Tuesday AM, or a fortnightly A/B week). You'll see yours in the app. If it's **locked** and needs changing, use **Submit change for approval** — it goes to the admin rather than changing on its own.

## Staying in the loop
- The **bell** shows your alerts — approvals, roster changes, and when self‑roster opens.
- You may also get an **email or text** when a round opens or the day before a shift (if your admin has reminders switched on).
- The **Help** tab has quick guides and answers any time you're unsure.
- **Send feedback** — *Help → 💬 Send feedback* goes straight to your roster admin.
- **Messages** lets you message the admin or the team.

## A few tips
- **Sign in on your phone and add it to your home screen** — then you're always one tap away, already logged in.
- **Add your shifts to your calendar** so you get reminders.
- **Book promptly** when self‑roster opens — but don't worry about being last; it's designed to be fair.
- **Questions?** Message your roster admin from inside the app.
