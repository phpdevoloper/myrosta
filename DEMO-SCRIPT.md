# Myrosta — demo video script (with voiceover)

**Goal:** a ~3–4 minute screen-recording that wins buy-in from **staff, admin and director**. Friendly, confident, benefit-led.

## How to record (easy)
- **Mac, free:** open **QuickTime Player → File → New Screen Recording** (record the whole screen or a window), tick the microphone so your voice is captured. Or use **Loom** (loom.com) — one-click record + instant share link.
- Before recording: open the app **already signed in as admin**, with your real data imported, and the cycle in **Draft** so you can show auto-draft live. Have a second browser/tab logged in as a **staff member** ("View as" works too).
- Speak slowly, pause between scenes. Aim for the timings below; it's fine to be ±20%.
- Tip: record each scene separately and stitch, or do one take — your call.

## Emphasis per audience (if you tailor versions)
- **Staff:** scenes at 0:45 and 1:15 — *fairness* and *shifts on your phone*. "Less hassle, fair go, your calendar."
- **Admin:** scene at 0:15 and 1:45 — *auto-draft* and *approvals with context*. "Hours become minutes; one place for everything."
- **Director:** scenes at 2:30 and 2:55 — *transparency, coverage, governance, near-zero cost*. "Fairer, safer, and effectively free."

## Quick checklist before you hit record
- [ ] Signed in as admin, real data imported, cycle in Draft
- [ ] A staff login (or "View as") ready for the self-roster + My shifts scenes
- [ ] One leave request already submitted (so you can approve it on camera)
- [ ] Microphone on; notifications/Slack silenced; clean browser (no personal tabs)
