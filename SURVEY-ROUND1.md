# Self-rostering vote — Round 1 survey (Microsoft Forms) · final wording

Paste-ready content for the Round 1 form. Round 2 (head-to-head) is drafted once the two finalists are known.

## Form settings

- **Who can respond:** Anyone with the link (no sign-in — decided 26 Jul 2026; "one response per person" is honour-system, sanity-check the response count against headcount at close).
- **Record names:** OFF (anonymous).
- **One response per person:** unavailable without org sign-in — covered by the description line.
- **Accept responses until:** [close date].

## Counting rules (published up front, restated with results)

- The two options with the most **first-choice** votes advance to Round 2.
- **Second choices** are used only to break ties.
- If someone picks the same option for both questions, only their first choice is counted.
- No demographic questions (EFT, fixed/flexible, zone) — they would de-anonymise a group this size.

---

## Form title

Self-rostering — how should we run it? (Round 1)

## Description

Anonymous — no names collected. One response per person, please. Takes about 2 minutes.
The five options are explained in the pack emailed to everyone. Your top two picks decide which two options go to a head-to-head next week. Counting rule: the two options with the most first-choice votes advance; second choices break ties. Nothing changes until the group backs it, and any change trials for one roster cycle with an open review.

## Q1 — Choice, required

**The current round has been described as feeling like a race — first in gets the pick. How much of a problem is this for you personally?**

- A big problem — it regularly affects the shifts I get
- A moderate problem — it sometimes affects me
- A minor niggle — it rarely affects me
- Not a problem for me

*(Purpose: measures how widespread the known issue is — the mandate meter. Not a re-ask of "what's wrong".)*

## Q2 — Choice, required

**Your FIRST choice — which option should we run?**

- Option A — Open booking (today's system): book your exact day, period and zone instantly; first in wins contested slots
- Option B — Turn-based: everyone picks in a rotating order, on your turn; order reverses each round
- Option C — Preference week + draw: mark your days across a week; clashes go to an open draw with priority carry-over for anyone who misses out
- Option D — Points budget: points follow your hours; put extra weight on the days that matter most; losing bids refunded in full
- Option E — Drafted for you: mark your preferences once; the system builds the whole roster with a visible reason for every allocation

## Q3 — Choice, required

**Your SECOND choice — pick a different option from your first.**

Same five options as Q2. *(Forms can't enforce "different from Q2" — the counting rule covers it.)*

## Q4 — Long answer, optional

**Anything we've missed? Especially a fairer idea we haven't thought of — a strong idea joins the shortlist. Dealbreakers and questions equally welcome.**

## OUTCOME (recorded 5 Aug 2026)

- **Option A (open booking — the current system) won; Option C (preference week + draw) was runner-up.** The group chose to keep first-come booking; no Round 2 was required and no change is being made.
- **Option C is the named plan-B**: fully specified in "Self-roster Fairness - Option C Explained.docx"; build only if the race complaint returns (compare against the Q1 "mandate meter" baseline from this survey).
- Results were fed back to staff and the loop closed (early Aug 2026).
- Build status at the time of the vote (verified in code): the app's per-cycle **Self-roster method** selector already offers A (Guarded free-for-all), B in two flavours (Snake-draft rounds, Cohort waves) and an E-lite (Auto-draft + adjust). C and D engines are design-complete, not built.
