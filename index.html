Questions, answered.

Is it fair?

Everyone books against the same open slots with the same rules: a share per cycle, fortnight caps, optional safe-hours rules and cover minimums. The Fairness tab shows who carried the tough shifts.

Does it work for nurses, junior doctors and clerks?

Clerks and any group on day and evening shifts, yes: each staff group has its own roster, its own zones and its own wording, under one hospital. Rostered night shifts are next to build, so 24-hour nursing and junior-doctor rosters wait for that.

Where is the data?

In a database hosted in Sydney. Sign-in is a one-time code by text or email; the services that deliver the codes are listed in the privacy policy. Every change is logged with who made it.

Does it talk to payroll?

It exports the roster and a one-row-per-shift payroll file in your template's columns, including for RosterOn.

What does it cost?

Early departments join on pilot terms while pricing settles. Book a walkthrough and ask.
