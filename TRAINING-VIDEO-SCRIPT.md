**How to use this file:** the **Say** column is the voiceover narration (paste straight into an AI‑voice tool). The **Show on screen** column is what to screen‑record. Production recipe is at the bottom.

## How to turn this into a narrated video (AI voiceover)

You don't need any video skills. The flow is: **record the screen → generate the voiceover from the script → line them up → export.**

**Recommended easy path — CapCut (free, Mac/Windows/phone):**
1. **Record the screen** doing the walkthrough silently, following the *Show on screen* column. On Mac: QuickTime → *File → New Screen Recording*. Do it in sections if easier.
2. In **CapCut**, drag your recording onto the timeline.
3. Add the narration: paste each **Say** block as a **Text‑to‑Speech** clip (CapCut → *Text → Text to speech*, pick a natural voice) — or add it as captions and let TTS read them.
4. Nudge the voice clips to line up with the matching on‑screen moment.
5. Turn on **Auto‑captions** for accessibility, add the Myrosta logo if you like, and **Export** as MP4 (1080p).

**For the most natural voice:** generate the narration in **ElevenLabs** (free tier) — paste the *Say* text, pick a voice, download the MP3 — then drop that audio into CapCut/iMovie over your screen recording.

**All‑in‑one alternative:** **Descript** (free tier) lets you record the screen, type the script, have an AI voice read it, and edit the whole video by editing text. Great if you'd rather not fiddle with a timeline.

**Tips:** keep each spoken line to one breath; pause ~1 second between scenes; record at a calm pace; do the product‑demo video (`DEMO-SCRIPT.md`) the same way.
