Support

Everything you need to get into Myrosta and use it day‑to‑day.

Signing in

  1. Go to myrosta.com.au and tap Sign in.
  2. Enter your work or personal email → tap Send code.
  3. Check your inbox for a 6‑digit code from Myrosta → type it in → Verify.
  4. You stay signed in on that device — it's a one‑time thing.

Add it to your phone

  • iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): ⋮ menu → Install app.

No code arriving?

  • Check your junk / spam folder.
  • Wait a moment, then tap Resend code (codes last about an hour).
  • Make sure you used your work or personal email, exactly as it's on file.
  • Hospital blocking external email? Ask your roster admin to add a personal email to your record — then sign in with that.
  • Still stuck? Your email may not be on the access list yet — contact your roster administrator.
Myrosta is administered by your department's roster lead (admin). For access, leave, swaps or roster questions, message the admin from inside the app (Messages) or contact them directly.
